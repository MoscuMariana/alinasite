This Cookie Policy explains how Alina Mihaela Stoian, trading as Italian Street Pasta, uses cookies and similar technologies on this website. By using our site, you agree to the use of cookies in accordance with this policy, where consent is required.

1. What are cookies?

Cookies are small text files placed on your device (computer, tablet, or phone) when you visit a website. They allow the website to recognise your device and remember things like your preferences. Cookies are widely used to make websites work, work more efficiently, and provide information to website owners.

2. Categories of cookies we use

Under the UK GDPR and PECR, cookies fall into two main categories:

  • Strictly necessary cookies — required for the website to function. No consent is needed.
  • Non-essential cookies — used for analytics, preferences, or marketing. These require your consent.

5. How to manage cookies

You have full control over the cookies we use on our website. You can:

  • Use our cookie banner — when you first visited our site, you were asked to accept or reject non-essential cookies. You can change your choice at any time by clicking the button below.
  • Use your browser settings — most browsers allow you to view, manage, and delete cookies. Visit aboutcookies.org for detailed instructions for each browser.
  • Use "Do Not Track" — most browsers offer this setting. We respect this signal where technically feasible.

6. What happens if you refuse cookies?

If you refuse non-essential cookies, the website will still function normally. Strictly necessary cookies (like the one that stores your preference choice itself) will still be set, as these are required for the site to work correctly.
