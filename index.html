Our Story

From the Heart of Italy
to the Streets of Basildon

What began as a passion for authentic Italian cuisine has grown into something more — a movement to bring the soul of Italy's street food culture to every plate we serve. Our journey didn't start in a classroom, but in the bustling kitchens of Italy, where we spent decades perfecting our craft.

With 20 years of experience for Bogdan and 10 years for Alina working directly in the Italian culinary scene, we decided it was time to bring that genuine, sun-drenched flavor to the UK. We aren't just cooking; we are sharing a lifetime of heritage and expertise.

"La pasta non è un piatto, è un'arte." — Pasta is not a dish, it's an art.

From our kitchen to yours, every bowl tells a story. Come taste the difference that craftsmanship makes.
