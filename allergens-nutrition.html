Allergens & Nutrition

Full transparency for every dish. Find allergen info for our menu and build your own meal with our nutrition calculator below.

⚠
Important — please read

All our food is prepared in a kitchen where the 14 major allergens are handled (cereals containing gluten, crustaceans, eggs, fish, peanuts, soya, milk, nuts, celery, mustard, sesame, sulphites, lupin, molluscs). We take great care to avoid cross-contamination but we cannot guarantee that any dish is 100% free from any specific allergen.

If you have a serious food allergy or intolerance, please tell us before ordering. Our team will guide you to the safest options.

UK Food Law
